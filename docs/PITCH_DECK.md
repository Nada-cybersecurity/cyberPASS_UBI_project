# Pitch deck (12 slides)

Design: white slides, one idea per slide, Majorelle blue accent (#4B3FCB), IBM Plex Sans, screenshots from the live app. Speaker notes are one or two sentences each.

1. **Problem.** "Can you prove your cybersecurity and data protection?" The question that stalls African suppliers in European deals. *Notes: Africa and Europe are 14 km apart at Gibraltar; the compliance gap is much wider.*
2. **Who suffers.** Moroccan SaaS, IT outsourcing and BPO companies with 10 to 250 staff and no security team; and the EU buyers who must assess them. *Notes: both sides lose time; deals slow down or fail.*
3. **Solution.** AfriEU CyberPass: context-aware cybersecurity and compliance passport for Africa-Europe business. *Screenshot: landing page.*
4. **How it works.** Context, Applicability, Controls, Evidence, Risk, Action, Trust. *Visual: the 6-step sidebar.*
5. **Africa to Europe use case.** AtlasTech, Rabat: GDPR reaches it through contracts and transfers, not automatically; remote access from Rabat is a potential transfer; Law 05-20 flagged for review. *Screenshot: Requirements.*
6. **Live demo.** Evidence: policy is not proof. Fix first: MFA, access reviews, incident response. Passport. *Screenshots or a 20-second clip.*
7. **Differentiation.** Explicit sourced rules; design vs operation evidence; two-sided supplier and buyer workflow; built for SMEs. *Notes: we don't claim nobody does parts of this.*
8. **Business model.** Free, SME, Professional, Enterprise (EU buyers), expert services marketplace. *Notes: prices are hypotheses we are testing.*
9. **Market opportunity.** Qualitative only until sourced: Morocco's growing digital export and offshoring sector, EU rules (GDPR, NIS2, DORA) pushing security requirements onto suppliers. *Add figures only with a cited source.*
10. **Roadmap.** Now: MVP. Next 90 days: interviews, lawyer review, 3 pilots, backend with tenant isolation. Then: Tunisia and Senegal jurisdiction packs, SOC 2 mapping, signed passports.
11. **Founder fit.** Master's in cybersecurity with a cybersecurity management specialisation, GRC programme projects (ISO 27001 evidence audits, vendor risk decisions, breach governance), ISO 27005 risk work; based in Rabat; works in Arabic, Amazigh, French, English and German.
12. **Closing.** "AfriEU CyberPass turns cybersecurity compliance from a barrier to cross-border business into a business passport." *Live demo URL and QR code.*
