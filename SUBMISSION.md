## Project title
AfriEU CyberPass

## Plain-English project summary (875 characters, limit 1,200)

Moroccan and African tech companies selling to Europe keep hearing one question: can you prove your cybersecurity and data protection? Most don't know which Moroccan and EU rules apply, what evidence counts, or what to fix first. Consultants are slow and costly; GRC tools are built for large firms.

AfriEU CyberPass turns a company's context (customers, data, systems, data flows) into clear answers: which requirements may apply and why, what its documents really prove, which risks matter most, and a 30/60/90-day plan. The result is an EU Customer Security Passport it can share with buyers. European buyers can also assess African suppliers and send precise evidence requests.

Unlike generic checklists, every result comes from explicit, sourced rules, and the evidence check separates written policy from real implementation. It is a readiness tool, not legal advice.
